## Gaps (stock is weak here)

Modern **sportfishing charter action** (outriggers, fighting chair, Cabo weigh station, live marlin/dorado/wahoo catches) is scarce on Unsplash/Pexels. Most free “marlin” hits are vintage or paid iStock ads.

**Recommendation for launch quality:**
1. Use the Cabo/El Arco/marina set above for destination + hero atmosphere now.
2. Plan a half-day shoot on the NSF boat (or license 10–20 rights-cleared Cabo charter photos) for: billfish release, yellowfin/dorado on deck, cockpit action, crew, boat exterior with Cabo behind.
3. Species ID cards can use Commons/NOAA illustrations short-term; swap for real NSF catch photos later.
